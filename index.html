Our story

Founded in September 2026, savaML was established with the goal of bringing together students passionate about data science, algorithms, artificial intelligence, and computer science, providing them with an environment that fosters learning, collaboration, and intellectual growth.

The club's mission is to cultivate curiosity and enthusiasm for the field of artificial intelligence among students by organizing educational initiatives, interdisciplinary projects, and activities that encourage critical thinking, creativity, and innovation.

Through its initiatives, savaML aims to build a strong and engaged community while creating academic and professional opportunities for its members in the field of artificial intelligence. We aspire to equip students with the knowledge, skills, and mindset needed to embrace the challenges and opportunities of an increasingly technology-driven world.

SavaML holds weekly meetings where members explore fundamental and advanced topics in artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science, and algorithmic thinking. These sessions combine theoretical concepts with practical applications, fostering both technical knowledge and collaborative learning. More information about our curriculum and weekly activities can be found in the Lessons section.

Our platform also provides an interactive environment where students can apply their knowledge by solving AI-related challenges. Members can access problem statements, download the required datasets and resources, develop their solutions, and submit their work through the platform for automated evaluation and feedback. This hands-on approach encourages continuous learning while strengthening problem-solving and programming skills. These challenges are available in the Problems section.

In addition, SavaML offers a curated collection of educational resources covering areas such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Neural Networks, and related disciplines. These materials are intended to support independent study and encourage members to deepen their understanding beyond the weekly meetings.

Our long-term objective is to prepare students for national artificial intelligence competitions, particularly ROAI and ONIA, while fostering a genuine passion for research, innovation, and computational thinking.
